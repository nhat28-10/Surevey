SureVey Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

SureVey is a survey marketplace application that connects customers who create survey campaigns with collaborators who complete eligible survey tasks. This Privacy Policy explains how SureVey collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the SureVey mobile application and related web services.

Information We Collect

Depending on your role and usage, SureVey may collect the following information:

  • Account information such as name, email address, phone number, role, and profile details.
  • Survey campaign information such as campaign title, description, instructions, reward settings, deadlines, and campaign status.
  • Submission information such as answers, notes, proof URLs, confirmation codes, contact email, and review status.
  • Wallet and payment-related information such as balances, reward transactions, withdrawal requests, bank name, account name, and account number.
  • Technical information required to operate the app, such as authentication tokens, device/network request metadata, and error logs.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Display survey campaigns and allow collaborators to participate.
  • Allow customers to review submissions and approve or reject rewards.
  • Process wallet balances, rewards, payment verification, and withdrawal requests.
  • Send in-app notifications related to campaigns, submissions, payments, and wallet activity.
  • Improve service reliability, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.

Payment and Banking Data

SureVey may display payment QR information and process transaction confirmation through payment service integrations. Bank account information submitted for withdrawals is used only for payment and administrative review purposes. SureVey does not sell banking data.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud or abuse, process payments, or support platform administration.

Data Storage and Security

SureVey stores data through its backend services and database providers. We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect user information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

User Choices

Users may update profile information in the app where supported. Users may also contact the SureVey team through the official support channel provided in the app or app store listing to request assistance with account or data-related questions.

Children's Privacy

SureVey is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to use online financial or marketplace services. Users should use the app only if they are legally permitted to create an account and participate in the service.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the SureVey team through the official contact information shown in the app store listing or in the SureVey application.
